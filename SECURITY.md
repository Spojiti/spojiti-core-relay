# Security

Found a weakness in this code, or in Spojiti itself? Tell us privately first:

* **spojiti@spojiti.world**
* the policy and the thanks page: https://beta.spojiti.world/security.html#disclosure
* machine-readable: https://beta.spojiti.world/.well-known/security.txt (RFC 9116)

You never have to tell us who you are. Please do not open a public issue for
a security problem until it is fixed.

What is in scope here is what SCOPE.md says is here. The rest of Spojiti is
not published, and a report about it is just as welcome at the same address.
